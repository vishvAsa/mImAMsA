> death of Purva Mimamsa and the exegesis mechanisms it developed

Why do you think it died? It's routinely studied by uttara and ubhaya mImAMsaka-s.  

Of course no one studies without application - the same PM nyAya-s are used to make sense of upaniShats (not to mention proper performance of karmans).  
Furthermore, rAmAnujIya-s consider pUrva and uttara mImAMsa one single unit.

There are novel applications too. If you have *novel* scriptures, there can be *novel* application. For that matter appayya used mImAMsA to prove saguNa brahma is a rudra different from trimUrti-s; and vaiShNava-s the contrary.
